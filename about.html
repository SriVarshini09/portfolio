About

I’m an Artificial Intelligence master’s student focused on building production-grade ML systems across document intelligence, semantic retrieval, and real-time ML platforms. I combine strong model development (NLP/CV, LLM pipelines) with engineering fundamentals—clean data workflows, rigorous evaluation, and cloud-native deployment. What motivates me is building systems that are useful in the real world: reliable, measurable, and clear to operate.

Research-to-production mindset

I like starting from a clear problem statement and ending with something that can be shipped: a tested pipeline, a service boundary, and a monitoring plan. I’ve built and productionized pipelines on AWS using EKS, SageMaker, and cloud-native tooling.

Systems thinking for scalability

I care about latency, throughput, and operational clarity—whether it’s optimizing retrieval, benchmarking LLM choices, or building feature stores that serve multiple models consistently.

Professional engineering fundamentals

I build with disciplined engineering practices: CI/CD, observability, versioned artifacts, and reproducible training pipelines—so ML remains maintainable as the system evolves.
